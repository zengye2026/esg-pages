Chapter Map · 1.1.5

先抓住隐藏线：钱 → 公司 → 规则

1.1.5 的核心不是“背三条”，而是把投资过程看成三层控制：先控制投资决策，再用所有权影响公司，最后影响市场规则。

💼
1. Investment Decisions
管钱去哪
ESG 从 RFP、manager appointment、mandates、monitoring 进入价值链，再进入 SAA、security selection 和 portfolio management。
题眼：RFP · appointment · consultants · mandate · monitoring · SAA · filter · threshold · thematic
🗳️
2. Corporate Engagement
管公司怎么做
投资者通过 AGM voting 或会外 dialogue / collective initiative，要求 investees 改善 ESG practices。
题眼：AGM · voting · resolutions · dialogue · board · management · collective initiative
🏛️
3. Policy Engagement
管规则怎么设
投资者与 regulators、standard setters、consultants、stock exchanges 等合作，让金融系统更稳定、更公平、更有效地纳入 ESG。
题眼：policy consultation · regulators · standard setters · level playing field · proper functioning
Logic Equation · 题目拆解
“Putting ESG Investing into Practice” == Practice 不是口号，而是 钱 的安排、公司 的改变、规则 的塑造。
题目中的 “investment process” 本身就是目录：投资过程先发生在投资决策中；持有后通过 engagement 影响公司；当单家公司层面不足以解决系统问题时，就通过 policy engagement 改变市场规则。
🧠 心象 1：投资桌

你坐在投资桌前：先发 RFP、选 manager、看 consultant 的三项评分，然后把 ESG 写进 mandate 和 monitoring，最后分配 SAA、挑证券。

🧠 心象 2：股东会

你拿着投票卡进入 AGM，投 resolutions；会后走进会议室，与 board / management 对话，也可能加入 collective initiative。

🧠 心象 3：监管大厅

你走进监管大厅，与 regulators、standard setters、stock exchanges 对话，回应 consultation、参与倡议、给 policymakers 建议。

三条路径Institutional investors typically reflect ESG considerations in three ways: investment decision-making, corporate engagement, policy engagement.这不是三种产品，而是三条实施层级：组合内、公司上、系统外。问 three ways / typically reflect ESG。
混合方式Different institutions take different approaches and blend these elements differently, reflecting their culture and investment style.同一三分法可以被不同机构按风格重新组合。问 why approaches differ。
RFP / AppointmentAsset owners can include ESG factors in requests for proposal and consider them in their appointment process.ESG 先进入选经理的入口，不只是进入买股票的环节。RFP / appointment = manager selection。
ConsultantsInvestment consultants can factor in asset managers’ ESG policy, implementation, and outcomes in selection.顾问看三件事：政策、执行、结果。policy / implementation / outcomes。
Mandate / MonitoringAsset owners integrate ESG views into mandates and monitoring processes.mandate 是把信念写成任务书；monitoring 是检查有没有兑现。views implemented。
SAAAsset owners and some asset managers embed ESG into SAA, allocating capital across asset classes, sectors, and regions based on return/income needs and risk appetite.SAA 是大类、行业、地区层面的资本分配。asset classes / sectors / regions。
Security selectionAsset managers and direct-investing asset owners incorporate ESG into security selection and portfolio management.这一层进入具体证券和组合管理。security selection / portfolio management。
Ratings filterUsing ratings to apply a filter or threshold rules potential investments in or out of the investment universe.rating 不只是打分，而是变成准入门槛。filter / threshold / universe。
Financial & riskIntegrating ESG issues in financial and risk analysis.把 ESG 作为财务与风险分析的一部分。financial analysis / risk analysis。
ThematicUsing ESG criteria to identify opportunities through a thematic approach, e.g. water fund, impact investing.ESG 也可以是找机会的主题线。water fund / impact investing。
AGM votingInvestors can encourage ESG improvements via AGM by voting on resolutions.股东会投票是正式表达观点的渠道。AGM / voting / resolutions。
Outside AGMEngagement can happen outside AGM, individually or through collective initiatives, discussing ESG with board or management.AGM 之外是对话：单独或集体；对象是董事会或管理层。collective initiative / board / management。
Policy rationaleProper functioning of markets and public policy affects investors’ ability to generate sustainable returns and create value.政策互动的底层理由是市场功能，而不是单纯游说。proper functioning / sustainable returns。
System designInvestors work with regulators, standard setters, consultants, stock exchanges to design a sound, stable system, level playing field, and ESG decision-making.政策互动的对象是规则制定生态，目标是系统更稳定、更公平、更能纳入 ESG。regulators / standard setters / level playing field。
Policy actionsInvestors respond to consultations, participate in collective initiatives, and make recommendations to policymakers.三种动作：回应、参加、建议。consult / join / recommend。

Exam Traps

题目陷阱：把相似动作分清

本节最容易错在把 CSR、corporate engagement、policy engagement 混成一团。

Practice Q19

Institutional investor 的 sustainable returns 不是靠披露自己的 CSR

A. 不 engage companies on ESG matters
B. disclosing corporate social responsibility activities
C. integrating ESG factors into portfolio construction decisions

正确线：portfolio construction + corporate engagement + policy engagement。CSR disclosure 是公司/机构自己说“我做了什么”，不等于 ESG factors 被纳入投资过程。

Practice Q26

Policy engagement 的理由是金融市场正常运转

A. asset owners need regulators to level the playing field
B. ESG matters can contribute to proper functioning of financial markets
C. policy consultations are mandatory

level playing field 是 policy engagement 的目标之一，但题目问“为什么 institutional investors most likely engage with policymakers”，更底层理由是 proper functioning of financial markets。

AGM vs Policy

投票不是政策互动

AGM、voting、resolutions、board、management 都指向 Corporate / Shareholder Engagement；regulators、standard setters、stock exchanges、policy consultations 才指向 Policy Engagement。

Self Test

自测题

先闭眼回忆“钱 → 公司 → 规则”，再展开答案。搜索框可筛题眼。

看到 RFP / appointment / consultant，该归到哪条路径？
Investment Decisions。它们发生在 manager selection，即投资价值链前端。
Consultant selection process 三个检查维度是什么？
ESG policy、ESG implementation、ESG outcomes。
SAA 的三个分配维度是什么？
Asset classes、sectors、regions。依据是 return/income need 与 risk appetite。
ratings filter / threshold 的作用是什么？
把潜在投资 rule in 或 rule out of the investment universe。
water fund 和 impact investing 在本节中作为哪个方法的例子？
用 ESG criteria 通过 thematic approach 识别投资机会。
AGM voting on resolutions 属于哪条路径？
Corporate / shareholder engagement。它是通过股东会正式表达观点。
Outside-AGM engagement 可以如何发生？
由 investment firm individually 进行，或 through a collective initiative 进行。
Corporate engagement 的互动对象是谁？
Investee company’s board 或 management。
Policy engagement 的底层理由是什么？
市场和公共政策的 proper functioning 会影响 institutional investors 生成 sustainable returns 和 create value 的能力。
Policy engagement 可以与哪些对象合作？
Regulators、standard setters，以及 consultants、stock exchanges 等 other parties。
Policy engagement 想设计什么样的金融系统？
More sound and stable、level playing field、better brings ESG into financial decision-making。
Policy engagement 的三个具体动作是什么？
Respond to policy consultations、participate in collective initiatives、make recommendations to policymakers。
Practice Q19 的陷阱是什么？
披露 institutional investor 自己的 CSR 活动，不等于把 ESG 纳入投资方式；正确线是 portfolio construction + corporate/policy engagement。
Practice Q26 的陷阱是什么？
不是 policy consultations mandatory；正确理由是 ESG-related matters 有助于 financial markets 的 proper functioning。
