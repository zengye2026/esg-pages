从政策目标到投资动作

本节表面是各国法规清单，隐藏线是“目标—规则—监督—披露—投资者保护”。只要看到一个名词，就先问它在链条里负责哪一层。

Policy == Paris / SDGs / net zero / national priorities
Regulation == disclosure + stewardship + asset owners + investor protection
Supervision == risk management + stress tests + capital requirements + reporting
Other stakeholders == disclosure/data + funding direction + pressure + evidence

Hidden Line

隐藏线：政策不是法规，法规不是监督

这节最重要的句子是：financial regulation downstream from policy choices；financial supervision downstream from regulation。它是所有国家清单的总目录。

Step 1

Policy choices

高层目标与政策选择，例如 Paris Agreement、SDGs、net zero、public infrastructure、low-carbon transition。

Step 2

Financial regulation

把目标写成 rules：corporate disclosure、stewardship、asset owner duties、investor protection。

Step 3

Financial supervision

把规则落实为 supervision：risk management expectations、stress tests、capital requirements、reporting。

Step 4

Market behavior

公司披露、资产所有者整合、产品更透明、投资者更能识别 sustainability risks and benefits。

目标

Regulators 三大目标

  • Maintain orderly financial markets。
  • Safeguard investments、savings/pensions and investment vehicles。
  • Bring about orderly expansion of financial sector activities。
四主题

ESG regulation 四门课

  • Corporate disclosure：公司披露。
  • Stewardship：投资者与被投公司互动。
  • Asset owners：养老金/保险等整合并披露。
  • Investor protection：透明度与市场效率。

Global Standards

TCFD / ISSB：全球披露语言

TCFD 是气候相关财务披露建议的核心框架，ISSB 的 IFRS S1/S2 把 sustainability-related disclosures 推向全球基线。

TCFD recommendations
Pillar 1

Governance

组织如何治理 climate-related risks and opportunities。

Pillar 2

Strategy

气候风险/机会对 business、strategy、financial planning 的实际与潜在影响。

Pillar 3

Risk management

识别、评估和管理 climate-related risks 的 processes。

Pillar 4

Metrics and targets

用于评估和管理 relevant risks and opportunities 的指标与目标。

IFRS S1

General sustainability disclosure

General Requirements for Disclosure of Sustainability-Related Financial Information：要求披露 sustainability-related risks and opportunities。

IFRS S2

Climate-related disclosures

专门要求 entities disclose information about climate-related risks and opportunities。题眼：ISSB 在 2023 年发布 S1/S2。

CVA · ISSB submission · Sustainable Finance Taxonomy

CFR 协调；APRA stress test；ASIC/CFR ISSB 反馈；ASFI taxonomy。

New Zealand

mandatory climate disclosures · comply-or-explain · FMA

2023 起强制气候相关披露；不能披露必须解释；FMA 监测执行。

United States

SEC proposal/final rule · Scope 1/2/3

Proposal 覆盖 Scope 1/2/3；2024 final rule did not include Scope 3。

国家房间走法：Europe = 五件套；UK = net zero law + PRA + TCFD/SDR/TPT；China/Singapore/ASEAN = taxonomy + risk/disclosure；Australia/NZ/US = stress test/disclosure/Scope。
Fiduciary Duty

Fiduciary duty：同一概念，既可成阻力也可成驱动

考试不是问你所有法律细节，而是问“受托责任是否允许/要求考虑 ESG”。核心判断：financially material ESG factors 逐渐被纳入 fiduciary analysis。

US

ERISA / DOL

  • ERISA private retirement plans；prudent-person standard。
  • Duties：sole interest、care/skill/diligence、diversification。
  • 1994 ETIs all-things-equal；2015 ESG may have financial value；2020 narrowed；2022 final rule allows climate/ESG when risk-return relevant。
UK

Law Commission / Stewardship Code

  • Trustees may and should consider financially material ESG。
  • Non-financial factors：members share concern + no significant financial detriment。
  • UK Stewardship Code 2020 Principle 7 integrates stewardship, investment, material ESG and climate。
EU

Shareholder Rights Directive II

  • Came into force in 2019。
  • Improve level/quality of investor engagement with investees。
  • Requires engagement policy and annual reporting on strategy integration, dialogue, voting rights, collaboration and conflicts。
逻辑等式：Fiduciary duty challenge == narrow interpretation blocks ESG ↔ financially material ESG makes ESG consideration permissible/required。

这一组不再是“监管者”，而是 ESG market quality 的后半段：谁提供数据、谁推动资金方向、谁施压、谁提供研究证据。

题目拆解

Policy + Regulation + Supervision

隐藏线Policy 领走目标与方向；Regulation 领走规则主题；Supervision 领走监督工具。
Policy choice → Financial regulation → Financial supervision
钩子：先有政府的方向盘，再有监管的交通规则，最后有交警执法。
隐藏线

Why regulators matter

隐藏线监管不是 ESG 市场外部噪音，而是把 policy market driver 变成可执行要求的桥。
ESG market driver == policy urgency + regulation + supervision + disclosure
钩子：市场需求拉动，监管把拉力变成义务。
四主题

ESG financial regulation

隐藏线Corporate disclosure 管公司说什么；Stewardship 管投资者怎么互动；Asset owners 管谁把 ESG 放进流程；Investor protection 管产品与客户透明。
ESG regulation == Disclosure + Stewardship + Asset owners + Investor protection
钩子：说清楚、管互动、管资产所有者、保护投资者。
Exhibit 10

目标到工具

隐藏线高层目标不会直接落地，必须经过 policy options，形成 primary purposes，再进入 toolkit。
Goals → Policy options → Purpose → Taxonomy / Disclosure / Risk regulation / Supervisory expectations
钩子：屋顶是目标，电梯到工具箱。
TCFD

四支柱

隐藏线TCFD 不是评级框架，而是披露框架；四支柱对应董事会、战略、流程、指标。
TCFD == Governance + Strategy + Risk management + Metrics and targets
钩子：谁管？怎么变业务？怎么管风险？用什么数字管？
EU

五件套

隐藏线EU 把投资价值链从公司报告到产品报告和标签全部连接。
EU sustainable finance chain == CSRD + Taxonomy + SFDR + Ecolabel + ESRS
钩子：公司说影响，分类说绿色，基金说产品，标签说声明，标准说怎么报。
EU Taxonomy

绿色活动条件

隐藏线先做贡献，再不伤害，最后守底线。
Environmentally sustainable activity == substantial contribution + DNSH + minimum safeguards
钩子：有贡献、不拆台、守底线。

Practice Traps

官方题眼陷阱

本模块最可能考：EU Taxonomy、comply or explain / comply and explain、financial regulators、investor protection、TCFD、CGT。

Q3 · EU Taxonomy

EU Taxonomy

看到 established a framework for economic activities to be environmentally sustainable，答案是 EU Taxonomy Regulation。

不要选 TCFD；TCFD 是披露建议，不是活动分类框架。
Q5 · comply and explain

comply and explain

Comply and explain = 更强、更成熟、通常 mandatory。

不要把它看成比 comply or explain 更宽松。
Q6 · financial regulators

financial regulators

最可能做的是考虑 ESG 对 long-term risk–return profile 的影响。

Regulators 不是 fiduciaries，也不是代表受益人做 collective engagement。
Q7 · stakeholders

stakeholders

Government 可通过 policy/regulation/services/information 直接影响投资决策。

Local community leaders / retail customers 不是教材这组直接 stakeholder 答案。
Q21 · comply or explain

comply or explain

允许被监管者解释为什么不遵守，因此会留下挑战 ESG integration 是否 required 的空间。

Mandatory disclosure 与 comply and explain 更强，不是 comply or explain。
Q22 · Taxonomy condition

Taxonomy condition

Doing no significant harm to other objectives 是三条件之一。

不是贡献至少三个目标，也不是最高 social/governance standards，而是 minimum safeguards。
Q23 · stock exchanges

stock exchanges

最能支持 ESG investing 的方式是提高 listed companies/securities 的 ESG disclosure requirements。

交易所不是 proxy voting provider，也不是 ESG rating agency。
Q27 · investor protection

investor protection

Investor protection regulations aim to protect investors by improving transparency and efficiency in markets。

不是限制信息，也不是阻止纳入 sustainability preferences。
Q28 · TCFD

TCFD

TCFD 四支柱是 Governance、Strategy、Risk management、Metrics and targets。

Credit ratings 不属于 TCFD 四支柱。
Q29 · CGT

CGT

Common Ground Taxonomy aims to establish shared understanding of what constitutes green economic activity。

不要答成单纯减碳或只提高透明度。
Self Test

自测反推：看题眼能不能回到逻辑式

建议先全部收起，逐题说出逻辑等式，再展开答案。搜索框可筛 TCFD、Taxonomy、Scope、DOL、NGFS 等题眼。

Q1Policy、regulation、supervision 三者的顺序是什么？
Policy choices 先决定方向；financial regulation 是 policy choices 的下游；financial supervision 又是 regulation 的下游。
Q2Financial regulators 的三大目标是什么？
Maintain orderly financial markets；safeguard investments/savings/pensions/investment vehicles；bring about orderly expansion of financial sector activities。
Q3为什么 sustainability risks 会进入金融监管？
因为 climate change、biodiversity、resource scarcity 等会影响 financial system stability，也会影响 individual portfolio risk。
Q4ESG-related financial regulations 的四大主题是什么？
Corporate disclosure、stewardship、asset owners、investor protection。
Q5Corporate disclosure regulation 的目的是什么？
要求或鼓励 investee companies 披露 material ESG risks，从而提高 investors 在 investment decisions 中考虑这些风险的能力。
Q6Stewardship regulation 管什么？
管 investors 与 investee companies 的 interactions，目的是保护 shareholders、beneficiaries 以及 market health/stability。
Q7Asset owner regulation 通常聚焦谁？要求什么？
主要聚焦 pension funds and insurers，要求 integrate ESG factors 并 disclose process and outcome。
Q8Investor protection regulations that consider sustainability 的目标是什么？
Protect investors by improving transparency and efficiency in markets。
Q9Exhibit 10 的一条主线怎么背？
High-level sustainability goals → high-level policy options → primary purposes → tools: taxonomies、disclosure/accounting standards、risk regulation/stress tests/capital requirements、supervisory statements/reports/research。
Q10TCFD 四支柱是什么？
Governance、Strategy、Risk management、Metrics and targets。
Q11IFRS S1 与 IFRS S2 分别管什么？
IFRS S1 是 sustainability-related financial information 的 general requirements；IFRS S2 是 climate-related disclosures。
Q12EU sustainable finance 的五件套是什么？
CSRD、EU Taxonomy、SFDR、EU Ecolabel、ESRS。
Q13CSRD 的 scope 条件是什么？
企业满足三项中的两项：EUR40m net turnover、EUR20m assets、250+ employees；non-EU companies 若在 EU 有 presence 且 EU turnover > EUR150m 也需 comply。
Q14EU Taxonomy 的三个条件是什么？
Substantial contribution to at least one environmental objective；do no significant harm to the others；comply with minimum EU social/governance safeguards。
Q15EU Taxonomy 六个 environmental objectives 是什么？
Climate mitigation、climate adaptation、water/marine resources、circular economy、pollution prevention/control、biodiversity/ecosystems protection/restoration。
Q16SFDR 的核心作用是什么？
标准化 financial products 的 sustainability reporting，提高透明度以防止 greenwashing，并识别 principal adverse impacts。
Q17UK 的 Climate Change Act / PRA 线索怎么背？
2008 Climate Change Act；2019 net zero commitment into law；PRA 2019 对 regulated firms 发布 climate-related supervisory expectations。
Q18UK Green Finance Strategy 的三个 areas 是什么？
Greening finance、financing green、capturing the opportunity。
Q19UK TCFD roadmap、SDR、TPT 的逻辑是什么？
TCFD roadmap 把披露强制化；SDR 做产品标签、披露和反 greenwashing；TPT 做 transition plan disclosure framework。
Q20China 绿色金融政策的三条线是什么？
2016 Guidelines for Green Financial System；PBoC 2021–25 strategy 支持 green loans/bonds/insurance/derivatives；CGT 与 EU Commission 对齐绿色活动理解。
Q21Common Ground Taxonomy 的目标是什么？
建立 shared understanding of green economic activities，并简化 cross-border green capital flows、降低 transaction/verification costs、减少 fragmentation。
Q22CGT eligibility criteria 如何确定？
采用两套 taxonomy 都有的标准，或采用更严格的标准。
Q23AMAC 与 CSRC 在中国线索中分别是什么？
AMAC 2018 Guidelines for Green Investment；CSRC 2021 ESG disclosure guidelines，主要为 voluntary reporting on climate、biodiversity、pollution、waste。
Q24Singapore MAS Green Finance Action Plan 的两条 taxonomy 工作线是什么？
一条是 Singapore-based sustainable finance taxonomy；一条是与 ASEAN member countries 的 multilateral collaboration。
Q25Singapore regulatory/supervisory measures 有哪三类？
Environmental risk management；sustainability-related disclosures；sustainability stress tests。
Q26ASEAN Taxonomy 与 EU Taxonomy 的关键区别是什么？
ASEAN uses multi-tiered approach；EU uses binary approach。ASEAN includes Foundation Framework and Plus Standard。
Q27Australia sustainable finance regulation 的核心动作是什么？
CFR coordination；APRA 2022 CVA climate stress test for large banks；ASIC/CFR ISSB submission；industry-led Australian Sustainable Finance Taxonomy。
Q28New Zealand climate-related disclosures 的规则是什么？
2023 起对 in-scope companies mandatory on comply-or-explain basis；披露 governance、risk management、strategies；不能披露则 explain；FMA 负责 monitoring/enforcement/guidance/reporting。
Q29US SEC 2022 proposal 与 2024 final rule 的 Scope 题眼是什么？
Proposal included Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 if material；final rule on 6 Mar 2024 ultimately did not include Scope 3 disclosures。
Q30Global combined effect 的三条转变是什么？
Comply or explain → comply and explain；voluntary → mandatory disclosures；policy → implementation and reporting。
Q31NGFS 是什么？
Network for Greening the Financial System，140 central banks and supervisors，established in 2017；recognizes climate risks as relevant to supervisory mandate。
Q32US ERISA 下 fiduciaries 的三个基本 duties 是什么？
Act solely in participants/beneficiaries’ interest；invest with care/skill/diligence；diversify to minimize large losses。
Q33US DOL fiduciary duty timeline 如何压缩？
1994 ETIs all-things-being-equal；2008 collateral factors rare/documented；2015 ESG may relate to financial value；2020 non-financial objectives/narrow rule；2021 non-enforcement/allow ESG and proxy voting；2022 climate/ESG may be considered if risk-return relevant。
Q34UK Law Commission 对 financially material ESG 的结论是什么？
Trustees may and should take into account financially material ESG factors。
Q35UK Law Commission 对 non-financial factors 的两个 tests 是什么？
Trustees have good reason to think scheme members share the concern；decision does not involve significant financial detriment。
Q36EU SRD II 要求 investors 做什么？
Have an engagement policy and annually report how it is integrated, dialogue carried out, voting/shareholder rights executed, collaboration with shareholders, conflicts managed。
Q37Investees 包括哪些实体？
Companies、projects、agencies、jurisdictions。
Q38Investees 对 ESG investing 的关键贡献是什么？
Manage ESG risks, manage environmental/social impact, and decide level of ESG disclosure to investors。
Q39Governments 认为 responsible investment 可服务哪三件事？
Pension/social security transition；public infrastructure funding；low-carbon economy transition requiring capital shifts。
Q40Civil society / NGOs 做什么？
Push company-level sustainability, demand transparency and consideration of investment impact, partner with firms/regulators or expose insufficient actions。
Q41Academia 的作用是什么？
Validate business case and conduct studies on ESG factors, integration into investment decisions, investment returns and financial markets。
Q42Q3/Q22/Q28/Q29 四个硬题眼如何反推？
Q3 EU Taxonomy framework；Q22 DNSH；Q28 TCFD not credit ratings；Q29 CGT shared understanding of green economic activity。
Compressed Review

压缩背诵版

考前最后 5 分钟只看这一页。

1 · 主链

Policy choices → financial regulation → financial supervision。政策定目标，监管写规则，监督查执行。

2 · 四主题

Corporate disclosure + stewardship + asset owners + investor protection。说清楚、管互动、管资产所有者、保护投资者。

3 · TCFD / ISSB

TCFD = governance + strategy + risk management + metrics and targets。IFRS S1 = general sustainability; IFRS S2 = climate。

4 · EU

CSRD + Taxonomy + SFDR + Ecolabel + ESRS。Taxonomy 三条件：substantial contribution + DNSH + minimum safeguards；六目标按气候、水、循环、污染、生物多样性背。

5 · 全球效果

Comply or explain → comply and explain；voluntary → mandatory；policy → implementation/reporting。NGFS = central banks/supervisors recognize climate risk as supervisory mandate。

6 · 题眼

Q3 EU Taxonomy；Q5 comply and explain；Q6 long-term risk–return；Q21 comply or explain；Q22 DNSH；Q27 investor protection；Q28 no credit ratings；Q29 CGT shared understanding。

最终一句：ESG regulation == 把 sustainability policy targets 翻译成 disclosure、taxonomy、risk supervision、investor protection 和 stakeholder action。
